=== the horizon this cannot see past ===
  index_recall_after is snapshotted when the 60s completeness window
  closes. So the supported claim is 'at ~60s, completeness has healed
  and index_recall has not' -- a statement about a HORIZON, not about
  permanence. #37 is the precedent: Qdrant graph damage that a 50s
  window called permanent was gone by 180s. A long-quiesce re-run is
  the experiment that would settle it, and it has not been done.
